Privacy Policy

Email Sender · Last updated: 20 October 2026

Who we are

Email Sender is a private tool built and operated by Taimoor Raza (Taimoor Raza Web Studio, Lahore, Pakistan). It is used only by its owner to send his own business emails from his own Gmail account, taimoorz146@gmail.com. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Google permissions we ask for

  • Send email on your behalf (gmail.send). Used only to send the emails that the owner has written and chosen to send or schedule.
  • Your email address (userinfo.email). Used only to show which Google account is connected.

The app cannot read, list, change or delete any email, contact or other data in the Google account.

What data we store

  • The access credentials (OAuth tokens) of the owner’s own Google account. They are kept on the owner’s computer and as encrypted secrets in a private code repository that runs the scheduled sending.
  • The text of the emails the owner writes, and the business contact details (company name and public business email address) of the people the owner chooses to write to. They are kept in the owner’s private files and private repository.

How we use and share data

Data is used only to send the owner’s own emails. We do not sell data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with anyone, except that Google (to deliver the email) and GitHub (to host the private repository that runs the scheduled sending) process it as service providers.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Email Sender’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The stored credentials and files can be deleted by the owner at any time. To ask a question or request deletion, write to taimoorz146@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version and date will be published on this page.
