Email Sender

Email Sender is a private tool that Taimoor Raza (Taimoor Raza Web Studio, Lahore, Pakistan) uses to review, schedule and send his own business emails from his own Gmail account.

What it does

  • Shows the owner a list of business contacts and the email written for each one.
  • Lets the owner edit an email, then send it now or schedule it for a later time.
  • Sends the email through the Gmail API from the owner’s own account, taimoorz146@gmail.com.

Google permissions

The app asks for one permission: to send email from the connected account (gmail.send). It also reads the account’s email address so that it can show which account is connected. It cannot read, list, change or delete any email in the mailbox. Only the owner’s own account is connected. The app has no other users and is not offered to the public.
